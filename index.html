About

Who I am: I'm an Economics (formerly Computer Science) student at DLSU-Taft. I love games, I love technology, and I really love AI. I think that business is about putting your idea out there and seeing whether people find value in it, so here are my ideas, and here I am putting them out there.

What AgenticPH is: More than just a brand, it's an idea: that these advanced systems and technologies are just as much for the average person as they are for big Fortune 500 companies or highly specialized experts. Artificial Intelligence doesn't have to be resigned to being a job-killing, resource-consuming, inhuman replacement. I know from personal experience that it can be a highly effective tool to innovate, learn, and help the people around you when it is used well.

Why I'm doing this: I am doing this because I know how hard even the simplest of these technologies can be to understand, and because I think I can bridge the gap between understanding AI and using it effectively.

How this works: I use my experience with agentic AI, systems thinking, and data analysis to turn abstract ideas into practical applications, then teach other people how to understand and use them. This isn't about being a frontier AI researcher; it's about knowing how to use these powerful tools to turn complex ideas into something people can use.
