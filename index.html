Hello viewer, this is Nathaniel (really me, not an AI), the person behind AgenticPH.

Who I am: I'm an Economics (formerly Computer Science) student at DLSU-Taft. I love games, I love technology, and I really love AI. I think that business is about putting your idea out there and seeing whether people find value in it, so here are my ideas, and here I am putting them out there.

What AgenticPH is: More than just a brand, it's an idea: that these advanced systems and technologies aren't just for the big Fortune 500 companies or highly specialized experts, they are for the people. Artificial Intelligence doesn't have to be resigned to being a job-killing, resource-consuming, inhuman replacement. I know from personal experience that it can be a highly effective tool to innovate, learn, and help the people around you when it is used well.

Why I'm doing this: I am doing this because I know how hard even the simplest of these technologies can be to understand, and because I think I can bridge that gap between easily understanding AI and effectively using it.

How this works: I have an idea, I use agentic AI to help me build it, then I teach other people to do the same. That simple. This isn't about being a frontier AI researcher, it's about making powerful tools that are practically applied and easy to understand.
