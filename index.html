About

We build practical AI systems for Philippine businesses — Economics & CS meets production engineering.

AgenticPH Labs builds practical AI tools for market intelligence, document analysis, business automation, and research decision support. Every project here is designed to ship — deployed via Streamlit Cloud, delivering working URLs, not repos to clone.

Our work spans procurement intelligence, customer feedback analysis, competitive monitoring, and structured research-to-decision pipelines. We focus on the gap between raw AI capability and real business utility — bridging it with clean architecture, actionable outputs, and observable results.
